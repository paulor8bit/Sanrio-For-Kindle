# Prompts das artes

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. Os originais são preservados em `originais/`. Os recortes e o empacotamento para o jogo são reproduzíveis pelo script `tools/prepare_sprites.py`.

+Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite sheet for a monochrome Kindle virtual companion game, matching chunky Game Boy / Pokemon Mystery Dungeon pixel art. Output one 1024x1024 transparent PNG, exact invisible 4 columns by 5 rows grid, every cell 256x~205. Eighteen individual full body sprites, same character scale and consistent baseline within cells, generous transparent padding; no overlap, no border, no labels or text, no scenery. Each character should occupy only central 150x145 pixels of its cell. Pixel art with crisp stair-stepped thick dark outlines, solid white interiors (white character is NOT transparent), only black, white and 2 gray tones, no color, no gradients, no blur or antialiasing. Front / slight three-quarter view. Row 1: 4 subtle idle frames (normal, blink, gentle sway left, gentle sway right). Row 2: 4 walk frames (left step, center, right step, center). Row 3: 4 playful happy waving/dancing frames, no weapons. Row 4: 4 eating frames, holding small cookie near face with slight bites. Row 5: first 2 cells sleeping frames (curled peacefully, breathing), last 2 cells completely empty transparent. Keep identities and outfit identical in all 18 frames. Character: Hello Kitty, recognizable Sanrio white cat with six whiskers, black oval eyes, small oval nose, NO mouth, signature bow on her left ear (viewer's right), simple dark-gray overalls over pale shirt.
